The catch

The loop introduced a bug, then caught it

Round 1 gave every timeline card a “Corriger la lecture” door with no role check. A reader — grandma, the godparent, half the accounts on a shared carnet — would open the full editor, pick a suggestion, watch the text change on screen, tap “Republier”, and receive:

« accès refusé — rien n'est perdu : vos corrections sont toujours à l'écran »

A promise that is false for her forever. The flow critic measured it: two wasted taps and a deterministic dead end, 100% of readers, every card, every evening. Round 2 added web/src/lib/access.ts — one client-side definition of write permission, deliberately stricter than the server (unknown role → reader), wired into the journal, capture and review.

Method

Two critics caught their own instrument lying

The contrast audit regex-extracted three numbers from a colour string, so oklab(0.4816 0.0192 0.0982 / 0.7) was read as rgb(0.48, 0.02, 0.10) — near-black. Every color-mix() and oklab() value, which is what every /NN opacity modifier compiles to, was therefore unmeasurable, and the invariant was reported clean while it was failing. Fixed by resolving colours through a canvas.fillStyle round-trip.

Blindness was enforced mechanically: the two images are shuffled into a.png and b.png, the mapping is written to a file, the critic commits its judgement in writing, and only then reads the mapping. A verdict written after the reveal does not count.
